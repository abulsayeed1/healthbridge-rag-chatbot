Your Hospital Stay
Hospitals can sometimes seem overwhelming, especially when you are ill. The following resources were developed to help you prepare for your hospital stay, to inform you of all the services and facilities we offer and to make your stay with us as smooth and comfortable as possible.
MyChart
We encourage you to sign up for MyChart, our online patient portal, if you haven’t already. MyChart offers an easy, confidential way to reach your health care team and access your medical information online.
